# 5 Business Insights & Recommendations

Derived from the KPI, region, and product analysis in `KPI_Summary_Report.xlsx`.
Every figure below is pulled directly from the workbook's formulas.

---

### 1. Furniture is dragging down overall profitability

Furniture is the only category with a negative profit margin overall (**-2.5% margin on
$742K of revenue, $18.5K profit**). Within it, **Tables lose -$17.7K on $207K of revenue
(-8.6% margin)** and **Bookcases lose -$3.5K (-3.0% margin)**, while Technology and most of
Office Supplies stay solidly profitable. The revenue is real — customers are buying this
furniture — so this is a pricing/discounting problem, not a demand problem.

**Recommendation:** Cap or restructure discounting specifically on Tables and Bookcases,
review supplier cost on these two sub-categories, and consider bundling low-margin Furniture
with high-margin Furnishings/Storage rather than discounting it standalone.

---

### 2. Discounts above roughly 20% are eating all the profit

Profit margin is a healthy **29.5%** on full-price orders and stays positive up to about a
20% discount. Past that point it flips negative:

| Discount band | Profit margin |
|---|---|
| 0% (no discount) | 29.5% |
| 0–10% | 16.6% |
| 10–20% | 11.6% |
| 20–30% | **-10.0%** |
| 30–50% | **-24.8%** |
| 50%+ | **-119.2%** |

Orders discounted 50% or more are being sold well below cost.

**Recommendation:** Set an internal approval gate at 20% discount and treat anything above
that as an exception requiring manager sign-off, rather than a routine sales lever.

---

### 3. Central region is the weakest performer despite solid revenue

Central generates **$501K in revenue** (3rd highest of four regions) but only **7.9% profit
margin — the lowest of all four regions** — versus 14.9% in the West and 13.5% in the East.
The gap traces back to discounting: **Central's average discount is 24.0%, roughly double
every other region (10.9%–14.7%)**. Central-Furniture is the single worst region/category
combination in the business, at **-1.8% margin**.

**Recommendation:** Audit Central's discount-approval process and sales-rep incentives, and
bring its average discount down toward the East/West level before pushing for more Central
revenue growth — more revenue at today's discount level would just mean more losses.

---

### 4. Revenue is growing faster than profit — margin has been flat-to-declining since 2016

Revenue grew every year from 2015 to 2017 (**+29.5% in 2016, +20.4% in 2017**), and profit
grew alongside it. But profit margin, which improved from **10.2% (2014) to 13.4% (2016)**,
has since slipped to **12.7% in 2017** even as revenue kept climbing. Growth is starting to
come at the cost of profitability.

**Recommendation:** Track profit margin — not just revenue — as a monthly scorecard metric
going forward, and investigate whether the heavier discounting seen in Furniture and Central
is what's diluting the 2017 margin.

---

### 5. Technology, especially Copiers, is the strongest performer and is under-leveraged in Central and South

**Copiers carry a 37.2% profit margin**, the best of any sub-category in the business, and
Technology is the strongest category in every region — including Central, where it still
leads at 19.8% margin but on far less revenue than Furniture or Office Supplies. West and
East already lean into Technology; Central and South have room to grow it.

**Recommendation:** Shift a portion of Central and South's marketing/inventory investment
from Furniture toward Technology (particularly Copiers and Accessories, 25.1% margin) to
lift regional profitability without needing to acquire new customers.
